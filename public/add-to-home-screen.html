Put the Observer App on Your Phone

About 2 minutes. Nothing to download. Nothing to pay for.

There is nothing to download.

We call it "the app," but it is really a website. You open it in the same program you use to look things up on the internet (Safari, Chrome, or Samsung Internet).

To make it feel like an app, you put a shortcut icon on your home screen. After that, you tap the icon and it opens, just like any other app.

You will not find it in the App Store or Google Play. If you see something there with a similar name, it is not ours. Do not install it.

First: open the website

Tap this address, or type it exactly into the address bar of your web browser:

web-trainstorm.onrender.com

The address bar is the long box at the top (or bottom) of the browser that shows the current web address. Typing it into a Google search box also works. Just tap the result that shows this exact address.

⏳ The first time, it can take up to a minute to appear.

If nobody has used it for a while, the website has to "wake up." A blank or loading screen for 30–60 seconds is normal. Wait. Don't keep tapping. After it wakes up, it's fast.

Did you tap a link in a text, email, or Facebook?

Sometimes the website opens inside that other app instead of your real browser. If so, you won't see the "Add to Home Screen" choice.

Fix: look for a button that says Open in Safari, Open in Chrome, or Open in browser (often behind ⋯ or ⋮). Or just type the address into your browser yourself.

Next: pick your phone

Not sure which you have? An iPhone has an Apple logo on the back. Almost every other phone (Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola, and others) is an Android.

🍎 iPhone or iPad, using Safari most iPhones

Safari is the browser with the blue compass icon. It is the easiest way on an iPhone.

  1. Open the website in Safari (see "First: open the website" above).
  2. Find the Share button. It's a square with an arrow pointing up.
    • Newer iPhones: tap the ⋯ (three dots) next to the address bar first, then tap Share.
    • Older iPhones: the Share button is right on the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, tap View More or More at the bottom of the list.
  4. If you see a switch called Open as Web App, leave it ON (green).
  5. You can rename it (for example, "Observer"). Then tap Add in the top-right corner.
  6. Go to your home screen. The new icon is there. From now on, always open it from this icon. (See "Keep your notes safe" below for why.)
🍎 iPhone, using Chrome, Edge, or Firefox

This works on newer iPhones. If anything below doesn't match your screen, the simplest fix is to use Safari instead (section above).

  1. Open the website in your browser.
  2. Tap the Share button. In Chrome it's usually at the right end of the address bar. If you don't see it, tap ⋯ and look for Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
🤖 Android, using Chrome Pixel, Motorola, most Androids

Chrome is the round red, yellow, green, and blue icon.

  1. Open the website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots stacked up and down) in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll down the menu. Tap the choice that says one of these (it depends on how up to date your phone is):
    • Install and create shortcut
    • Add to Home screen
  4. If it asks you to choose, tap Create shortcut.
  5. You can rename it (for example, "Observer"). Tap Add.
  6. If it asks again, tap Add again or Add automatically. You can also drag the icon to where you want it.
🤖 Samsung phone, using Samsung Internet

Samsung Internet is the purple icon with a planet. Many Samsung phones use it by default. (If your Samsung uses Chrome instead, use the Chrome section above.)

  1. Open the website in Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the ≡ (three lines) at the bottom-right.
  3. Tap Add page to.
  4. Tap Home screen.
  5. Tap Add. If it asks again, tap Add once more.
🤖 Android, using Firefox or Edge
  1. Open the website in your browser.
  2. Tap the menu: ⋮ in Firefox, or ≡ / ⋯ at the bottom in Edge.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (in Edge it may say Add to phone). You may need to tap More first to see it.
  4. Tap Add. If it asks again, tap Add once more.
💻 Computer (Windows or Mac)

Most observing happens on a phone, but a computer is handy for reading the training.

  1. Open the website in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).
  2. Save it as a bookmark: press Ctrl + D on Windows, or ⌘ Command + D on a Mac. (Or click the ☆ star at the right end of the address bar.)
  3. Click Done. Next time, find it in your bookmarks or just start typing "web-trainstorm" in the address bar.

Keep your notes safe

Your observations are saved on your phone until you tap "Submit to team." Until then, they live only inside the browser you used. Four rules:

  1. Always open it the same way, from your home-screen icon. On an iPhone, the icon and Safari keep separate notes. Something you logged in Safari will not show up in the icon, and the reverse.
  2. Don't use Private or Incognito browsing. Everything is erased when you close it.
  3. Submit before you clean up. Don't clear your browsing history, "website data," or cookies, and don't delete the home-screen icon, while you have unsent observations.
  4. Submit at the end of every shift, even if you plan to come back.

Already logged things in the browser before you made the icon? Open the browser the old way one more time, tap Submit to team, and confirm it went through. Then switch to the icon for good. Codes you entered on the Setup tab may need to be typed again in the icon.

Helping someone else set up?

  • Have them open it in their real browser, not from a text-message preview.
  • Make the icon before they log anything.
  • Have them close the browser tab and open the icon once, so they know where it lives.
